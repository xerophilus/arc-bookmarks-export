Arc Browser Shutdown Timeline

On September 25, 2024, The Browser Company announced Arc will be sunsetted to focus on Dia, their new AI-first browser. Here's everything you need to know.

Arc stops working December 22, 2026. After this date, you won't be able to open or use Arc. Back up your data now.

Official timeline

Source: Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect

September 25, 2024

Arc sunset announced. The Browser Company unveiled Dia and announced Arc's end-of-life.

October 1, 2024 – December 14, 2026

Maintenance period. Arc continues to work. Security updates and critical bug fixes provided, but no new features.

December 15, 2026

Last day to request account data. Final day to request a complete export of your Arc account (browsing history, settings, synced data) from The Browser Company. After this date, account data exports are unavailable.

This doesn't affect your ability to manually back up StorableSidebar.json or use Arc's File → Export Data, which work locally.

December 22, 2026

Arc stops working. Arc will cease to function. You won't be able to launch or use the browser. All sync services shut down.

What happens to your data

What you keep

  • Local files: StorableSidebar.json and other local files remain on your computer, but you can't open them in Arc
  • Exported data: Any Markdown exports or bookmark conversions you've made continue to work
  • Imported bookmarks: If you've moved bookmarks to another browser, they're safe

What you lose

  • Arc sync: Cloud-synced data (tabs, settings) deleted from The Browser Company's servers
  • Account data: After December 15, 2026, you can no longer request your full account export
  • Browser functionality: Arc won't open after December 22, 2026

What to do now

  1. Back up StorableSidebar.json — Contains your complete sidebar. Find it here
  2. Export from Arc — Use File → Export Data for a Markdown list
  3. Request account data (if needed) — For browsing history and synced settings, request before December 15, 2026
  4. Convert bookmarks — Use the converter to create bookmarks.html for any browser
  5. Choose a new browser — Popular for Arc users:
    • Dia (free Arc import included)
    • Zen Browser (modern, Firefox-based)
    • Chrome
    • Firefox
    • Safari (Mac)

Why is Arc shutting down?

The Browser Company is focusing on Dia, a new AI-first browser designed to "browse for you." Rather than maintaining two browsers, they decided to sunset Arc and offer migration tools to Dia.

Arc was loved for Spaces, vertical tabs, Split View, and clean design. Many concepts are being reimagined in Dia with AI capabilities.

Questions

Can I use Arc after December 22, 2026?
No. Arc will stop functioning on that date.

Will Arc get security updates until December 22?
Yes. The Browser Company committed to security updates and critical fixes during maintenance.

What if I don't export before December 22?
Your local files (StorableSidebar.json) remain on your computer, but you can't open Arc. Back up now.

Can I sync between devices?
Yes, until December 22, 2026. After that, sync services shut down.

Are there free export options?
Yes. Arc's File → Export Data is free (Markdown). Dia offers free Arc import. For other browsers, this converter is $7.
