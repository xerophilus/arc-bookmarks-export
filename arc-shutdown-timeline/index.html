Arc Browser Shutdown Timeline

On September 25, 2024, The Browser Company announced that Arc browser will be sunsetted to focus development on Dia, their new AI-first browser. Here's everything you need to know about Arc's shutdown and key dates.

⚠️ Arc stops working on December 22, 2026. After this date, you will not be able to open or use Arc. Back up your data now.

Official Timeline

Source: Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect (The Browser Company)

September 25, 2024

Arc sunset announced. The Browser Company publicly announced Arc's end-of-life and unveiled Dia, their next browser.

October 1, 2024 – December 14, 2026

Maintenance period. Arc continues to work normally. The Browser Company provides security updates and critical bug fixes, but no new features are being developed.

December 15, 2026

Last day to request account data. This is the final day you can request a complete export of your Arc account data (browsing history, settings, synced data) from The Browser Company. After this date, account data exports will no longer be available.

Note: This does not affect your ability to manually back up StorableSidebar.json or use Arc's File → Export Data feature, which work locally on your computer.

December 22, 2026

Arc stops working. On this date, Arc will cease to function. You will not be able to launch or use the browser. All sync services will shut down.

What Happens to Your Data?

✅ What You Keep

  • Local files: StorableSidebar.json and other local Arc data files remain on your computer after the shutdown, but you won't be able to open them in Arc.
  • Exported data: Any Markdown exports or bookmark conversions you've made will continue to work.
  • Imported bookmarks: If you've already moved your bookmarks to Chrome, Firefox, or another browser, they're safe.

❌ What You Lose

  • Arc sync: Cloud-synced data (tabs, settings across devices) will be deleted from The Browser Company's servers.
  • Account data: After December 15, 2026, you can no longer request your full account data export.
  • Browser functionality: Arc will not open after December 22, 2026.

What You Should Do Now

  1. Back up StorableSidebar.json — This file contains your complete sidebar structure. See where to find it.
  2. Export your data from Arc — Use File → Export Data to get a Markdown list of your bookmarks.
  3. Request your account data (if needed) — If you want a complete export of browsing history and synced settings, request it before December 15, 2026 through Arc's support.
  4. Convert your bookmarks — Use the Arc Bookmarks Converter to create a standard bookmarks.html file you can import into any browser.
  5. Choose a new browser — Popular choices for Arc users:
    • Dia (The Browser Company's new browser, includes free Arc import)
    • Zen Browser (modern, Firefox-based)
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari (Mac only)

Why is Arc Shutting Down?

The Browser Company is focusing its resources on Dia, a new AI-first browser designed to be "the browser that browses for you." Rather than maintaining two browsers, they decided to sunset Arc and offer migration tools to Dia.

Arc was beloved by its community for features like Spaces, vertical tabs, Split View, and clean design. Many of these concepts are being reimagined in Dia with AI capabilities.

Is Dia free? Yes, Dia is free and includes built-in Arc import at no cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still use Arc after December 22, 2026?

No. According to The Browser Company's official announcement, Arc will stop functioning on that date.

Will Arc get security updates until December 22, 2026?

Yes. The Browser Company committed to security updates and critical bug fixes during the maintenance period (October 2024 through December 2026).

What if I don't export my data before December 22?

Your local files (like StorableSidebar.json) will remain on your computer, but you won't be able to open Arc to view or export your bookmarks. Back up your data now to avoid losing access.

Can I still sync Arc between devices?

Sync works until December 22, 2026. After that, all cloud sync services will be shut down.

Is there a free way to export Arc bookmarks?

Yes. Arc's built-in File → Export Data gives you a Markdown file for free. However, browsers can't import that format directly — you need to convert it. Dia offers free Arc import. For other browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari), this converter is a paid tool ($7).
